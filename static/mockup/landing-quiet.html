Colour rule
One rule worth keeping now that he is yellow: yellow is his and nothing else's. No button, badge or highlight on the site uses it. Purple stays the product's colour and rides on his hat, so the two read as related without competing. It is why the “hear him teach” strip above is grey and not gold.

What it is

Not a video, not a worksheet, not a chatbot

He teaches out loud

A warm voice explaining, and a whiteboard filling in beside it, step by step. Your child answers on the board or the keypad — the microphone stays off.

He never just gives the answer

A wrong answer gets a question back, then a smaller question, then a worked example of a different problem. The one thing he will not do is finish it for them.

The whole journey, not one grade

Ten courses under one subscription, from first counting through differential equations. Placement at the start, mastery quizzes along the way, and a child who never outgrows their tutor.
