What he actually does

Not a homework-answer machine.

He asks before he tells

Every step is a question your child answers. When they are stuck he makes the step smaller — he never just fills it in.

He writes it down

Nothing is left to imagine. If he says it, it appears on the board, in order, and a wrong line never stays up.

He plans the course

He knows what your child has mastered and what comes next, and he tells you both in plain words.

Three, not six. The live page carries six selling sections above the footer. A visitor who reads three remembers three.

How it works

Four steps.

Pick a course

Or let him place your child with a short, unmarked check.

Turn the sound on

He teaches out loud. Your child talks back, taps, or types.

Work a lesson

Twenty minutes is a good day. He stops before they are tired.

Read the report

You get what was learned and what is next, in a paragraph.

Safety

You can read everything
