The trust questions

The questions you should ask any AI tutor.

You're right to ask them. Here are ours, answered plainly.

"Will it just give my kid the answers?"

No. Mr. Cadabra teaches one step at a time and waits for your student's turn. Even a frustrated "just tell me" never gets a bare answer: after a genuine try, he works a problem on the board while explaining it — then immediately hands your student a similar one that's theirs.

"Can I trust the math?"

As Mr. Cadabra teaches, a separate math engine re-computes his checkable claims before your student sees them — and every score is plain arithmetic, never a judgment call.

"What happens to my child's voice?"

They talk with Mr. Cadabra back and forth; their speech is transcribed to text and the audio is deleted immediately — never stored. Need it quiet? Typing works any time, and elementary students can simply tap their answers. Parents & privacy →

"What if my child says something personal?"

Mr. Cadabra stays warmly on the math — he never pretends to be human and doesn't wander into chit-chat. If something bigger than math ever comes up, he responds with care in one short message and encourages your child to talk with you or another trusted adult.

The method behind the teaching: built on the What Works Clearinghouse practice guides where they exist (elementary math through algebra), and on professional standards — NCTM, MAA — for the courses above them.

Questions homeschool families ask

The honest answers, before you commit.

Can this be our entire math curriculum?▶

Yes. Ten sequenced courses from first-grade math through college, a placement that finds the right starting unit, mastery quizzes with a real 90% bar, and a visible scope & sequence for every course. Or keep the curriculum you love and use Mr. Cadabra alongside it — Practice mode coaches your student through any problem they bring.

Will it satisfy my state's homeschool requirements?▶

We give you the records states most commonly ask for: an instructional-hours log built from engaged time only, mastery evidence from real scored quizzes, and a printable scope & sequence and records report. States differ, and you stay in charge of your state's specifics — we hand you the paperwork, not legal advice.

How are instructional hours counted?▶

A minute counts only when the lesson is open and your student is actually working. Walk away, switch tabs, leave it running — the clock stops. The number that lands in your log is one you can file with a straight face.

How does it work with more than one child?▶

Each child gets their own login code, their own tutor memory, their own dashboard, and their own pace — all managed from one free parent account, where you can also rename a child or mint a fresh code. Plan details are on the pricing page.

Can I set what my child works on next?▶

Yes — from your parent account, pick the unit you want front and center and it becomes today's plan in the classroom. Your child's own deliberate choice is still respected in the moment, and their dashboard's "strengthen next" keeps the best next step obvious even when you don't steer.

What do I show an evaluator or co-op?▶

The printable records report — hours log, quiz scores, mastery evidence, and awards — is one click from your parent view. Course pages double as a printable scope & sequence for portfolios and curriculum filings.

My child is behind grade level — will this embarrass them?▶

No. Placement starts them where they actually are, and no grade level ever appears on their screen — an older student can rebuild fractions with complete dignity. The tutor meets them there and builds up.

Do I have to teach the math myself?▶

No — Mr. Cadabra does the teaching, out loud, one step at a time, starting from what a word even means. You get the window in: the parent view, the Friday email, and the list of recently tricky problems, so helping is easy when you want to lean in.
