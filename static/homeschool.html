Your window in

Watch your student's progress — without hovering.

Your parent code opens a read-only view of your child's real progress: what's mastered, what's slipping, and — most useful of all — "where to help next." No made-up numbers, no participation trophies: every score comes from an actual check your student took.

Teaching more than one child? Each gets their own code, their own dashboard, and their own pace.

Ownership

Students track their own journey.

Your student sees the same truth you do: a nine-unit path through each course, units turning gold as they're mastered, a day streak to keep alive, and "strengthen next" suggestions that make the next step obvious. Progress they can see is progress they chase.

Honest hours

We track time the way a homeschool log needs it — real work only.

Mr. Cadabra's Classroom counts a minute only when the lesson tab is actually open and your student is actually doing something. Walk away, switch tabs, leave it running — the clock stops. So "3h 59m this week" means three hours and fifty-nine minutes of math, a number you can put in an instructional-hours log with a straight face.

Every dashboard shows it: today, this week, per course. (Sample data.)
