The lesson

He talks. The board draws. You talk back.

Turn your sound on — Mr. Cadabra explains every step out loud while the whiteboard draws it. When it's your turn, tap the 🎙️ mic and say your answer out loud — a real conversation, back and forth. (Rather not talk? Type your answer and press Enter ⏎ instead.) Stuck? Just tell him "I'm confused" or ask for a hint — he gives nudges, never just the answer.

And here's the secret: wrong answers are useful. Mr. Cadabra never makes you feel bad about one — he gets curious about it. That's how the learning happens.

The board never runs ahead of you — it waits for YOUR answer.

Say it, type it, or plot it — he's listening either way.

Your tools

Talk it, type it, or plot it.

Talking is the main way — say "x equals four" or "two thirds" and he understands. Answers easier to write than say? Type them — x^2 and 3/4 work fine. And there's 📈 graph paper: tap points onto the grid and send them straight to Mr. Cadabra.

Not sure how something works? Just ask him. "How do I use the graph paper?" is a completely normal question — he knows every button.

Questions students ask

Good questions. Straight answers.

Do I have to talk out loud?▶

No. Talking is the quickest way, but you can type every answer instead — Mr. Cadabra treats both exactly the same. And the youngest students don't type at all: they tap big answer buttons.

What happens when I get one wrong?▶

First you get a hint. Miss again and he explains it a different way, working the steps right on the board. He never makes you feel bad about a wrong answer — he gets curious about it, because wrong answers show him exactly how to help you.

Will he just give me the answer if I keep asking?▶

No — and that's on purpose. He'll ask the next small question, drop hints, and re-explain, but you do the thinking. If you're truly stuck, he shows the way warmly and you move on together.

Can he help with my real homework?▶

Yes. Open Practice and bring the exact problem from school — he works through it with you, step by step, without just doing it for you. You leave knowing how, not just what.

What if I have to stop in the middle of something?▶

Your progress saves automatically — quizzes, badges, streaks, everything. Even a placement assessment picks up right where you stopped if you come back within a couple of days. Sign in with the same code on any computer, tablet, or phone and it's all there.

How do I earn the badges and trophies?▶

Score 90% or better on a Unit Quiz and that unit's merit badge is yours. Master all nine units and the course trophy goes in your trophy case. Medals reward the work itself — streaks, minutes, problems practiced — and once you've earned something, it never gets taken away. Rough quiz day? You can retake it when you're ready — your best real score is the one that counts.

What's a sprint?▶

A short, timed race through quick math facts — start one from your dashboard whenever you want. It's you against your own best score, and it's the fastest way to make the facts automatic.

What can my parents and teachers see?▶

Your real progress: units mastered, quiz scores, honest time, and which problems were tricky lately. Their view is read-only — nobody can change your work but you. And your voice is never recorded: what you say becomes text and the audio is deleted right away.
