The lesson

He talks. The board draws. You talk back.

Turn your sound on — Mr. Cadabra explains every step out loud while the whiteboard draws it. When it's your turn, tap the 🎙️ mic and say your answer out loud — a real conversation, back and forth. (Rather not talk? Type your answer and press Enter ⏎ instead.) Stuck? Just tell him "I'm confused" or ask for a hint — he gives nudges, never just the answer.

And here's the secret: wrong answers are useful. Mr. Cadabra never makes you feel bad about one — he gets curious about it. That's how the learning happens.

The board never runs ahead of you — it waits for YOUR answer.

Say it, type it, or plot it — he's listening either way.

Your tools

Talk it, type it, or plot it.

Talking is the main way — say "x equals four" or "two thirds" and he understands. Answers easier to write than say? Type them — x^2 and 3/4 work fine. And there's 📈 graph paper: tap points onto the grid and send them straight to Mr. Cadabra.

Not sure how something works? Just ask him. "How do I use the graph paper?" is a completely normal question — he knows every button.

Earn it

Merit badges, trophies, and medals — all real.

Master a unit (score 90%+ on its check) and you earn its merit badge — nine collectible badges per course. Master all nine and the whole course trophy goes in your trophy case at the top of your dashboard. And these medals reward the work itself:

🔥 Spark / Blaze / Unstoppable

Work 3, 7, or 30 days in a row

⏱ Century Club / 500 Club / Scholar

100, 500, or 1,000 minutes of real work — idle time never counts

✏️ First Ten / Workhorse / Centurion

Practice 10, 50, or 100 problems

🎯 Brave Start

Take your very first quiz

💯 Perfect Quiz

Score 100% on any quiz

💪 Bounce Back

Master a unit after a tough first Unit Quiz — the strongest one there is

🧭 Explorer

Work in two different courses

🗺️ Pathfinder

Complete a course assessment

Nothing here is a participation trophy — every one of these has to be earned. That's what makes them worth showing off.

Your progress

This is YOUR dashboard — trophy case and all.

Everything you earn lives at the top: your 🏆 trophy case with every medal (new ones get a NEW! tag), your merit-badge count, and the next award you're closest to. Below it, your nine-unit journey — units turn gold with a star when you master them — plus your 🔥 streak, your accuracy, and "Strengthen next" when you want the fastest win.
