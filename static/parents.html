What your child experiences

A tutor that teaches — and won't be talked out of it.

Mr. Cadabra explains each step out loud in a warm voice, draws it on a whiteboard, and asks the next question instead of handing over the answer — the way a good human tutor works. He never does the homework for them: the board waits for your child's answer at every step.

And he stays on task: Mr. Cadabra's Classroom is math only. He won't chat about games or gossip, won't discuss other students, and politely refuses every attempt to pull him off topic — including the creative ones kids try.

The board never runs ahead of the student.

The parent view — read-only, always current. (Sample data.)

What you see

Your own window, without hovering.

Your free parent account (at mrcadabra.com/family) opens a read-only view of each child: how they're doing, where to help next, real accuracy from real scored checks, and honest time-on-task — a minute counts only when your child is actually working, so "42 minutes today" means 42 minutes of math, not 42 minutes of an open tab.

📬 And once a week, the summary comes to you — see the preview below.

Included with every plan

📬 A weekly report in your inbox.

Every Friday: minutes of real work, units advanced, checks passed, new awards, and Mr. Cadabra's own written summary — the check-in a good tutor would give you, written automatically. Lands every Friday, with one-click unsubscribe.

Your family's data

Privacy promises we can keep.

🎙️ Voice audio never stored

Your child talks with the tutor — a real conversation. Their speech is transcribed to text and the audio is deleted immediately: no recordings, ever. Typing works any time too.

🚫 Never sold, no ads

We never sell student data and show no ads. We don't ask children for email, phone, photos, or location — a first name (any name you like) and a code is all it takes.

🔑 You hold the keys

Your free parent account creates each child's login code, shows you their progress, and manages the plan — and you can have your child's data deleted any time with one email.

The full plain-language policy is at Parents & Privacy — written to be read, not scrolled past.

Questions parents ask

Before you hand it to your child.

Can we try it before creating anything?▶

Yes — the demo lesson is a full interactive lesson with voice and the whiteboard, no account and no card. The parent walkthrough above shows you the dashboard the same way. Decide first, sign up second.

What does setting up actually involve?▶

About five minutes. Create your free account at mrcadabra.com/family, add each child with a first name or nickname, and each one gets their own login code. We never ask children for email, phone, photos, or location.

What will I see as a parent?▶

A read-only view of each child: units mastered, real accuracy from real scored quizzes, honest time-on-task, and which problems were recently tricky. Every Friday the summary also comes to your inbox in plain language — and you can switch the email off (and back on) right from your account.

Can I guide what my child works on?▶

Yes. From your account you can steer — pick the unit you'd like your child centered on, and it becomes today's plan in the classroom. One honest caveat: if your child deliberately picks something else that day, their choice is respected. It's guidance, not remote control — motivation matters.

Is my child's data safe here?▶

Voice audio is never stored — speech becomes text and the audio is deleted immediately. No ads, nothing sold, cookieless analytics, and a nickname is all we need. You can have your child's data deleted any time with one email. The plain-language policy is at Parents & Privacy.

What ages and levels does it cover?▶

Ten courses from first-grade math through college — Entry-Level Math all the way to Calculus and Differential Equations. A friendly placement finds the right starting point, and no grade level ever appears on the student's screen — an older child can rebuild fractions with dignity.

What if my child just tries to get answers out of it?▶

He won't hand them over. Mr. Cadabra is math-only, stays on task, and answers "just tell me" with the next right question, a hint, or a fresh explanation. Kids try — he's patient, warm, and immovable.

What devices do we need?▶

Any modern browser — computer, tablet, or phone (on a phone the whiteboard comes first and the mic docks at the bottom). Nothing to install. Headphones are nice for focus, never required.
