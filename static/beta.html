Beta program

Help shape Mr. Cadabra's Classroom — test it free.

We're polishing Mr. Cadabra's Classroom for launch, and honest feedback from real families is the most valuable thing there is. Approved beta testers get a personal pass to the full product — every course, the voice, the whole experience.

1

Ask us

Tap the button below — it opens an email to us that's already written. Just add your name and send. We approve testers personally and reply with your beta pass.

2

Get your pass

Your pass is a code like TRY-MAPLE42, good for 5 sign-ins. Each sign-in opens about a 2-hour session of full access. Use them across different days — your progress is saved between sessions — or even share one sign-in with another family.

3

Tell us the truth

What clicked, what confused, what's missing. Reply to our email or post in the Community — we read everything, and your feedback steers what gets built next.

Ready to test-drive it?

One email. No card, no account, nothing to install.

✉️ Apply to be a beta tester

The fine print, plainly: a beta pass is free and expects nothing but honesty. 5 sign-ins per pass, each opening a timed session (about 2 hours) with full access to all ten courses. Progress carries across sign-ins. When the pass is done, we'd love to hear what you thought — and if you want to keep going, the Free plan and pricing are right here.
