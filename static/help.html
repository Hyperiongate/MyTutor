Need a hand? You're in the right place.

Quick answers first. If your question isn't here, the grown-ups section at the bottom says exactly who to ask.

🔑 "How do I sign in?"

You need your login code — it looks like MAPLE42. Your parent has it (it's on their family page). Go to Student sign in, type the code, and you're in. No password — the code is the whole key. Lost it? Ask your parent to open mrcadabra.com/family; it's right under your name.

🎙️ "The microphone isn't working."

First tap the mic button once — your browser will ask "Allow microphone?" Tap Allow. If you tapped Block by mistake, click the little lock (or mic) icon in the address bar at the top and switch the microphone to Allow, then try again.

And remember: you never need the mic. Tap "Type my answer" and type instead — Mr. Cadabra treats typed answers exactly the same. In the younger courses you can also just tap the answer buttons.

🔇 "I can't hear Mr. Cadabra."

Check the sound switch on your device (and the volume keys). If you're on headphones or Bluetooth, give it a second — his first word sometimes waits for your speaker to wake up. If he's still silent, refresh the page; the lesson picks up right where you were. Nothing is lost when you refresh.

😵 "I'm stuck on the math."

Say (or type) "I'm confused." That's not giving up — it's a power move. It tells Mr. Cadabra to slow down and try a different way. You can also tap 📖 Look it up in a lesson to read about any topic, or tap "I'm not sure" when answer buttons are up.

🔁 "I closed the tab / my computer died."

It's fine. Sign back in and Mr. Cadabra remembers where you were — your progress, your quizzes, your trophies. Nothing disappears when a tab closes.

👪 For parents & teachers

Parents: your account at mrcadabra.com/family is mission control — each student's login code, progress, printable records, the weekly-email switch, and billing. The plain-language privacy promise is at Parents & Privacy.

Teachers & co-ops: your class view lives at mrcadabra.com/teacher.

A real person answers email: write to support@mrcadabra.com (open in your mail app) — questions, bugs, billing, data requests, anything. Students: if that button doesn't work on your school computer, just show a grown-up this address.
