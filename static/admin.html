🎵 Scripted course audio

      Renders every line the scripted course can ever speak into the
        voice cache. Safe to re-run — already-cached lines are skipped free, so after new
        lessons ship you only pay for the new lines. (kf: the count said "24 lessons" long
        after the course grew to 41 across two courses — it now says neither, because
        the button walks whatever the engine has.)

    

      
      
    

    

      ② only pays for lines that are not already cached. Lines already

Note: changing SCRIPT_TTS_MODEL changes every line's cache
      key, so after a voice change the whole course counts as missing again.
    

    

  


  
  

    
🔨 Voice cache repair

      Finds cached voice clips that are damaged — truncated, or two
        renders spliced together by the old cache bug — and removes them so they
        re-render clean. A child hears a damaged clip as slurring or nonsense. Checking is
        free and deletes nothing; run ② only after ① shows you the count.

    

      
      
    

    

  


  
  

    
🎧 Course audio quality

      Checks whether each cached clip is the right shape and a
        plausible length for the words it speaks — the things a perfectly valid mp3
        can still get wrong. Free and reads only. Then re-render a single lesson to
        replace clips that sound bad, or to try a different voice model without paying
        for the whole course.

    

      
    

    

      
      
      
      
    

    

      ④ re-renders every line of the chosen lesson, including ones already
      paid for, and replaces them. That is what it is for — a cached clip is
      never re-made otherwise, so this is the only way to fix one that sounds wrong.
      It asks you to confirm, and shows the real cost, before it spends anything.
      
Careful: with Whole course selected it re-renders all of it.
