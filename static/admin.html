🔨 Voice cache repair

      Finds cached voice clips that are damaged — truncated, or two
        renders spliced together by the old cache bug — and removes them so they
        re-render clean. A child hears a damaged clip as slurring or nonsense. Checking is
        free and deletes nothing; run ② only after ① shows you the count.
