Everything, on one page

What makes Mr. Cadabra different.

Not a video library. Not a worksheet machine. A patient, one-on-one teacher who talks, draws, checks his own math, proves mastery, and keeps parents in the loop — every feature below is live in the classroom today.

🧑‍🏫 The teaching

The heart of it: a real lesson with a real teacher — who happens to be tireless, endlessly patient, and available whenever your child is.

🗣️

A tutor who actually talks

Mr. Cadabra teaches out loud, in a warm human voice — your child listens like they would in class, then answers by typing or tapping. No microphone, ever: nothing records your child.

🖍️

A live whiteboard

He draws every step as he explains it — equations, number lines, shapes, graphs — and the board never runs ahead of the student. See it, hear it, work it.

🤝

Socratic, one-on-one

He doesn't lecture and he doesn't hand over answers. He asks the next right question, lets your child do the thinking, and steps in exactly when they need him.

🪜

Help that never dead-ends

Miss once — a hint. Miss again — a different explanation with the steps worked on the board. Still stuck? He shows the way warmly and moves forward. Struggle is normal here; being abandoned isn't.

🎯

Lessons tailored in real time

Pacing, examples, and encouragement adapt to the student in front of him — including per-topic playbooks for the classic misconceptions kids actually have.

👆

Built for every age

The youngest students tap big answer buttons — no typing needed. Older students get a full math keyboard (√, x², fractions) and graph paper the tutor can teach them to use.

📋 Your starting point & your plan

Ten complete courses on one ladder — and an assessment that finds exactly where your child belongs on it.

🧭

The Course Assessment

A friendly placement that maps strengths and weaknesses across the whole course, then recommends the right starting point — so no one sits through what they already know, or drowns in what they don't.

🗺️

A curriculum you can see

Every course is laid out unit by unit, topic by topic, right in the classroom. Students always know where they are and what's next — no mystery meat.

🚀

Ten courses, grades 1 to college

Entry-Level Math through Basic Math, Pre-Algebra, Algebra I & II, Geometry, Trig/Pre-Calc, Probability & Statistics, Calculus, and Differential Equations — one tutor, the whole journey.

✅ Proof, not promises

Anyone can say "your child is doing great." We built the machinery to prove it — and to catch our own mistakes.

🧮

Every answer math-checked

A symbolic math engine independently verifies the math in the tutor's replies — not "we're careful," but checked, every time.

⚡

Immediate, specific feedback

Right or wrong, your child hears exactly what was right and what to fix, in the moment — the kind of feedback that builds real confidence instead of empty praise.

📝

Two-tier quizzes

Topic quizzes (80% to advance) checkpoint progress inside every unit, and the Unit Quiz demands 90% for mastery. Progress here is earned, and it sticks between sessions.

📊

A dashboard that never lies

Units mastered, accuracy, streaks, engaged time — nothing is ever invented or inflated. If a number is on the screen, it happened. Empty means empty.

🏆

Awards that mean something

Trophies for mastery, medals for effort and streaks — earned awards never vanish — and the 🎓 Graduate honor when a whole course is truly finished.

⏱️

Honest time tracking

The clock counts engaged, working minutes only — idle time never pads the numbers. What you see is real learning time.

👨‍👩‍👧 For parents

You stay in the loop without hovering — and the paperwork takes care of itself.

📬

A weekly email that speaks parent

Every Friday: what your child worked on, where they shone, where they struggled — written in plain language, not data dumps. One-click unsubscribe, always.

🖨️

Printable homeschool records

Download and print a clean report — hours log, mastery evidence, quiz scores, awards — ready for state homeschool filing or your own records.

🏠

One family account

Add each child with just a first name or nickname; each gets their own login code, tutor, and dashboard. One plan covers up to two children. You see everything, read-only.

🏫 For teachers & co-ops

Follow a whole class without new logins, new software, or new headaches.

🧑‍🏫

Classrooms in one code

Gather your students under a simple class code — no accounts to build, no passwords to reset — and watch the whole group at once.

🌡️

The class heatmap

One glance shows every student against every unit — who's strong, who's stuck, and exactly where to spend your attention tomorrow.

🔍

Read-only student views

Open any student's honest dashboard — the same real numbers their parents see. Look, don't touch: teachers and parents can never alter a student's work.

🛡️ Community & trust

Built from day one for other people's children — which is the highest bar there is.

🔒

Privacy by design

No microphone, ever. First names or nicknames only — we ask for less data, not more. Analytics are cookieless. Your child is a student here, not a product.

💬

A parent community

A moderated forum where families share what's working, trade ideas and resources, and shape which courses come next.

🧪
