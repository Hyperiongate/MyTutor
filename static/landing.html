Questions

Frequently asked

Is this just ChatGPT that solves the homework?

No. MyTutor is built to teach. It uses a Socratic approach — guiding with questions and drawing each step on a whiteboard that never runs ahead of your student — wrapped around complete, placement-to-mastery courses. The goal is understanding, not a copied answer.

Does my child need a microphone?

No. Mr. Cadabra talks your student through each step in a warm voice, and your student answers by typing — with a built-in math keyboard for symbols like √ and x². There's no microphone, so no student voice is recorded: it's quiet-friendly, works on shared devices, and keeps things private.

What ages and levels is it for?

Today the math library spans eight complete courses — Pre-Algebra, Algebra I, Geometry, Algebra II, Trig / Pre-Calculus, Calculus, Differential Equations, and Probability & Statistics — and placement puts each student exactly where they belong. Our roadmap keeps growing toward a complete K‑12-and-beyond companion.

How do you handle my child's data?

Parents create and control the account. We never sell student data or use it for advertising. You can request deletion at any time, and you choose whether any voice features are used. See the Parents & Privacy page for specifics.

Can I use this for more than one child, or a co-op?

Yes — ask about family pricing for multiple students, or the teacher dashboard for classrooms and co-ops with rosters, a mastery heatmap, and assignments.
