Classrooms · co-ops · microschools

See your whole class at a glance.

Every student gets the same patient one-on-one tutor — and you get the classroom view: who's mastered what, who needs help, and where the whole class is stuck.

The class mastery heatmap — best check score per unit, gold star at 80%+. (Shown with sample data.)

📋 Roster by code

Add students to a class with their login codes — no student emails, no new personal data collected. Works for a family of three or a class of thirty.

🔥 Honest heatmap

Cell colors come from real scored checks, not participation points. "Needs attention" surfaces the unit your class is actually weakest on.

👤 Per-student drill-down

Click any student for their full dashboard: units, accuracy, streak, and honest time-on-task (idle time doesn't count).

📝 Course assessments

A voluntary, friendly assessment maps each student's strengths and recommends a path — foundation gaps first.

🎯 Meet them where they are

A 9th grader can quietly work in Pre-Algebra fractions without ever seeing a grade level on screen — built for intervention without stigma.

🔊 Voice out, typing in

Mr. Cadabra talks; students type. No microphones in your classroom, no student voice recorded — headphones are all you need.

Coming with launch

📬 Your whole class, summarized weekly.

Every Friday, one email with a summary for each student in your class: their real minutes, what they mastered, where they're stuck, and who needs you most this week. The Monday-morning picture, delivered before Monday. Included with classroom plans at launch.
