Questions teachers ask

The practical stuff, answered.

How do my students sign in?▶

With a simple login code — no student emails, no passwords to reset, no accounts to build. You gather those codes under your class code and the roster builds itself.

What does the class view actually show me?▶

The mastery heatmap: every student against every unit, colored by their best real quiz score, gold star at 90%+. "Needs attention" surfaces the unit the class is weakest on, and clicking any student opens their full dashboard — units, accuracy, streak, honest time.

Can I — or a parent — change a student's record?▶

No. Adult views are read-only by design. Every number comes from a quiz the student actually took, which is exactly what makes the heatmap worth trusting.

Do my students need microphones?▶

No. Headset mics make it a real conversation, but typing works everywhere — including a silent classroom — and the youngest students just tap answer buttons.

What does it cost for a classroom?▶

This term we're running free pilots with a limited number of classrooms, co-ops, and microschools — you get the product, we get honest feedback. Email support@mrcadabra.com and tell us about your class.

Will it work for my intervention students?▶

It was built for exactly that: no grade level ever appears on the student's screen, so a 9th grader can quietly rebuild Pre-Algebra fractions without stigma while the rest of the class works elsewhere.

What data do you collect on my students?▶

A first name or nickname and a code — that's it. No student emails, phones, or photos. When students talk with the tutor, speech is transcribed and the audio is deleted immediately, never stored. Nothing is sold and there are no ads.

Does it fit a co-op or microschool?▶

Yes — the class view works the same for three students or thirty, and setup is minutes, not meetings. Mixed ages and mixed courses in one roster are exactly what it's for.
