## 1. The scripted second explanation — the redo is a FRESH problem

After a first miss, the engine speaks its own worked solution of the problem the student
missed, then asks a **fresh problem of the same shape** from the bank — not the one they have
just seen solved. The AI enters only on a second consecutive miss. (Phase A of the scripted
second level; the AI's own redo path keeps the same problem, as today, because there the
student never saw a solution.)

(09-12: attempt 2 "overrides the student's request to stop", attempt 3 "should gently
encourage one more problem"). The standing "done for the day" conduct flag closes with the
