## The split rule

A generated walk-back must still open with the literal `Here it is, step by step: `, and
the text after the colon is pinned lower-case by the `wd`/`we` pins. So every split lands
at the **first natural stop after that phrase**, never at the colon:

- `yint`: "Here it is, step by step: at x equals zero, 3 times zero is zero. The whole times
  part vanishes, and all that is left is the plus 2."
- `rem`: "…7 shared into groups of 2 fills 3 groups — 3 times 2 equals 6. Then 7 take away 6
  leaves 1."
- `ntal`: "…5 percent of 80 is 4 out at the ends. The bell is symmetric, so they split
  evenly — 2 above two deviations and 2 below."

Every wrong-path note the old sentences carried is still spoken — `mside`'s "not 4 plus 3;
adding the same difference bends the shape", `rbet`'s "never halve", `ntal`'s "4 counts
both ends when the question asked for one", `vert`'s twin. Only the breath moved.
