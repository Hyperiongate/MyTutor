## 2 · PARENTS video (for mrcadabra.com/parents) — `Parents_Video_Deck.pptx`

**Slide 1 — You stay in charge.**
If you're a parent looking at yet another math app, here are the three things you actually want to
know: what your child experiences, what you get to see, and how your family's data is treated.
Let's take them in order.

**Slide 2 — A tutor that teaches — never just answers.**
First, what your child experiences. Mr. Cadabra teaches the way a good human tutor does — he
explains out loud, draws every step, and asks the next question instead of just giving the answer.
And he stays on task: math only, no matter how creatively your kid tries to change the subject.

**Slide 3 — Your own window in.**
Second, what you see. Your own parent code opens a read-only view of your child's real progress —
what's mastered, what's slipping, and most useful of all, where to help next. Every number comes
from an actual scored check. Nothing is invented.

**Slide 4 — Honest time, honest numbers.**
We even track time honestly. A minute only counts when your child is actually working — if they
walk away or switch tabs, the clock stops. So when the dashboard says forty-two minutes today,
that's forty-two minutes of real math. A weekly progress email is included with every plan at launch.

**Slide 5 — Privacy promises we can keep.**
And third — your family's data. There's no microphone, ever: the tutor talks and your child types,
so no student voice is recorded. We never sell student data and show no ads. And you hold the keys —
you control the account, and one email deletes everything.

**Slide 6 — Try it before your child does.**
The fastest way to judge MyTutor is to try it yourself — the sample lesson takes two minutes and
there's no sign-up. Visit mrcadabra.com, and if you have a question, a real person answers the email.
